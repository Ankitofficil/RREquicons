## 3.2 EPC Projects

### Hero
**H1:** End-to-End EPC. One Team. One Accountability.

### What is EPC?
**Engineering, Procurement, and Construction** is a turnkey contracting model where a single contractor takes responsibility for the entire project lifecycle — from design through commissioning. It's the model demanding clients prefer when they want certainty on cost, schedule, and quality.

### Why R R Equicons for EPC?
- **Single point of accountability** — no finger-pointing across vendors
- **Optimized design-build interface** — engineering decisions informed by execution reality
- **Faster timelines** — overlapping design and construction phases
- **Cost certainty** — fixed-price commercial models available

### Sectors We Serve via EPC
- **Roads & Highways** — state highways, district roads, urban arterials
- **Bridges & Culverts** — RCC, prestressed, steel composite
- **Industrial Buildings** — factories, warehouses, workshops
- **Commercial Buildings** — offices, retail, mixed-use
- **Residential** — townships, staff quarters, group housing
- **Water & Sanitation** — pipelines, treatment plants, drainage networks
- **Institutional** — schools, hospitals, religious complexes

### Our EPC Process

**1. Engineering & Design** — Concept development, feasibility studies, detailed engineering drawings, BOQ preparation.

**2. Procurement** — Vendor evaluation, material sourcing, quality verification, just-in-time logistics.

**3. Construction** — Mobilization, self-performed execution, multi-stage QA/QC, daily progress tracking.

**4. Commissioning & Handover** — Testing, snag-list closure, as-built documentation, training, warranty support.
